🔴CISCO NetFlow:
-----------------
🔹Network management protocols like SNMP allow us to monitor our network.
🔹we can check (CPU load, memory usage, interface status ....)
🔹We cant track all the flow like SNMP do.
🔹Allows us to track these flows on our network.
🔹We can use this informatio to solve problems like bottlenecks, identify what applications are used, how much BW they use etc...
🔹Configure router to keep track of all flows and export them to a cenral server.
🔹Is an application.
🔹Is a protocol developed by cisco systems to record all IP traffic flows.
🔹Flow characteristics are like (SRC, DST Port, Address, protocol, Type, etc...). ==> netflow allows tracking these flows on in the network.
🔹Collect and export data for network planning,
🔹UDP/2055,9555,9995

🔴In summary of NetFlow:
-------------------------
➤ One of the most important tasks of a network is to [monitor the health of our networks, learn how our BW is being used, what application are consuming it, when it needs upgrade...].
➤ SNMP or SPAN : can help us answer some questions but they are not enough to give us an insightful view of our networks.
➤ NetFlow: is a network analysis protocol that gives the ability to collect detailed information about network traffic as it flows through a router interface.
