🔴1.3.b Benefits and limitations of SD-WAN solutions:
#####################################################

🔴Benefits and limitations of SD-WAN :
--------------------------------------
🔹Simple
🔹Easy to manage
🔹Secure
🔹Scalable
🔹Efficient
🔹Low cost 
🔹Zero-touch provision
🔹Incrise agility : easy to add or remove links and change routing policies.
🔹Emprove performance : optimizing traffic flows and avoiding congested links.

🔴Limitions of SD-WAN :
------------------------
🔹Complexity :
	➤ complex to deploy and manage
	➤ 

🔹Vendor lock-in:
	➤ Some of vendors lock their customers ointo their own HW/SW.
	➤ difficult to switch vendors if needed.
	➤ chose SD-WAN vendor that offers a flexible architecture that can be easily integrated with other technologies.

🔹Heterogeneity :
	➤ its important to choose an SD-WAN vendor that offers a centralized management platform that can be used to manage all devices in the network.
