🔴 OSPF Load Balancing:
-----------------------
🔹Balancing of a traffic load across redundant links of equal cost.
🔹(ECMP) Equal-cost multi-path: 
	->is strategy used in routing to forward traffic to DST over multiple best paths.
	->Allows router to insert more than one path to DST in routing.
🔹Best paths have calculated the same metric.
🔹Occurs when the router receives a multiple paths with same AD.
🔹by default OSPF support of 4 paths 
	-> can be change to 32 oath max. --> cmd: maximum-paths 32
	R2(config)#router ospf 1
	R2(config-router)#maximum-paths ?
  	    <1-32>  Number of paths
	R2(config-router)#maximum-paths 5
