🔴Broadcast: Full mesh topology where all routers can communicate directly, with DR/BDR roles assigned.
🔴Non-Broadcast: Similar full mesh but with manual neighbor configuration (shown with dotted lines).
🔴Point-to-Multipoint: Hub and spoke topology with automatic neighbor discovery.
🔴Point-to-Multipoint Non-Broadcast: Hub and spoke with manual neighbor configuration.
🔴Point-to-Point: Simple two-router direct connection.
