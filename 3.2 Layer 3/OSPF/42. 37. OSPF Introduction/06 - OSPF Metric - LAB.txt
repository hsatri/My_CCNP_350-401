🔴OSPF Metric:
--------------
🔹OSPF used COST as a metric -> based on BW of an interface.
🔹Formula : COST = Referance BW / Interface BW
		Referance BW -> 10Mbit = 10^8
🔹The Lower cost is the best path.
🔹if two âths is equal cost -> ospf will use both paths and do a load balance .
	ex : f0/0 and f0/1 --> router will send 50Mbps via f0/0 and 50 via f0/1 to a DST.


🔴OSPF Cost Table:
------------------

| Link Bandwidth (bps)     | OSPF Cost  |
|--------------------------|------------|
| 1 Mbps                   | 100,000    |
| 10 Mbps                  | 10,000     |
| 100 Mbps                 | 1,000      |
| 1 Gbps (1,000 Mbps)      | 100        |
| 10 Gbps (10,000 Mbps)    | 10         |
| 100 Gbps (100,000 Mbps)  | 1          |

🔴Formula for OSPF Cost:
------------------------
OSPF Cost = 10^8 / Link Bandwidth (bps)
