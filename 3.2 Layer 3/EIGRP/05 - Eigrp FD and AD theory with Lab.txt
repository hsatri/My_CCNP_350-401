🔴EIGRP Terminologies : 
-----------------------
🔹AD(Advertised Distance)/(RD)Reported Distance:
	- The cost from the neighbor to the Destination :
		Ex : R1--R2--R3--L1
		-> R2 to L1 : AD = 15
🔹(FD)Feasible Distance: The total cost to reach a DST network: 
	Ex : R1--R2--R3--L1
		-> R2 to L1 : AD = 15
	so FD = (R1--R2) + AD = 5 + 15 = 20

🔴Successor : 
	- The primary route used to reach a DST.
	- Best route

🔴Feasible Successor :
	- The backup route.
