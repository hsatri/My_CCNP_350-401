🔴 OSPF Summarization:
----------------------
🔹also called route a aggregation.
🔹minimizing the Nmbr of routing tables in an IP network.
🔹select multiple routees into a single route advertisement.
🔹OSPF support route summarization only at ABR/ASBR.

🔴 Advantages of Summarization:
----------------------
🔹Saves memory
🔹saves BW
🔹saves CPU Cybles
🔹Stability -> prevents routing table instabilty due to flapping network.
