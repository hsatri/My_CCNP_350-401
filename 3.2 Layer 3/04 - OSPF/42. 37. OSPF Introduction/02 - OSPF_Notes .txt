🔴OSPF Packet Types:
--------------------
🔹Hello
🔹(DBD)Database Description.
🔹(LSR)Link-State Request.
🔹(LSU)Link-State Update.
🔹(LSAck)Link-State Acknowledgment.


🔴HELLO:
--------
🔹Neighbor Discovery.
🔹Building neighbor adjacencies and maintain them.


🔴DBD:
------
🔹is used to check if the LSDB between 2 routers is the same.
🔹is a summary of the LSDB.


🔴LSR:
------
🔹Requests specific link-state records from an OSPF neighbor.

🔴LSU:
------
🔹Sends specific Link-state records that were requested -> this msg is like an envelope with multiple LSAs on it.

🔴LSAck:
--------
🔹OSPF is a reliable protocol so we have a packet to acknoweldge the others.
