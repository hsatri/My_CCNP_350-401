🔴OSPF Address Family : 
-----------------------
🔹OSPFv2 : IPV4
🔹OSPFv3 : IPV6
	➜ Runs per-link rather than per-subnet.
	➜ Enables both IPV4/IPV6 unicast traffic to be supported.
	➜ Messages are send over IPV6 and therefore requires that IPV6 routing is enabled.
	➜ after configuring OSPFV3 families, show cmnds should be use.
	➜ commands to active (AF) : address-family ipv6
