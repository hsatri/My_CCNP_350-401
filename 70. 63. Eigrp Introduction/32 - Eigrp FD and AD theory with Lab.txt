no shut

en

no shut

no shut

🔴Eigrp Config : 
----------------
! R1 EIGRP Configuration
en
conf t
router eigrp 1
network 192.168.88.0
network 192.168.99.0
network 1.1.1.1
no auto-summary


! R2 EIGRP Configuration
en
conf t
router eigrp 1
network 192.168.88.0
network 192.168.99.0
network 2.2.2.2
no auto-summary
